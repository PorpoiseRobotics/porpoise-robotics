Setup — camera, field calibration, waypoints

Camera

This computer must be on the same WiFi network as the camera.

Field calibration

The origin is the bottom-left corner of the map. Stand on that corner facing down the long side with the field on your left; that compass heading is the bearing.

Destination

Marks a gold crosshair on the map. The Dest ΔX / ΔY readouts show the feet left to travel along each axis (+ means further right / up); they turn green within 10 ft.

Waypoints

Columns (first sheet): name, x_ft, y_ft — feet from the origin corner, like graph points — or name, lat, lon in GPS degrees. An optional sheet named field with key/value rows auto-fills the calibration.
